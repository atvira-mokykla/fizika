These are four original trial lessons for grade 9, not a completed thermal-physics course. Each has an English and Lithuanian text, projector mode, teacher notes and separate handout, worksheet and answer-key print views. Save a print view as PDF in your browser.

Practising physics-teacher review, independent Lithuanian terminology review, execution with real school equipment and classroom timing validation are still pending. Review explanations, misconception feedback, model limits, safety and print layout using the [review sheet](/fizika/review/lesson-review.csv).

For the investigation, the teacher prepares and checks water at 35–40 °C, never above 40 °C. Pupils do not heat water. Dry-run the protocol with your actual cups and thermometer, following school procedures. Synthetic data serve only to practise analysis; blank tables are for actual readings.

The [grade 9 thermal topic](/fizika/9/thermal/) shows available and planned lessons, their year-plan placement and official programme scope. Registry IDs are not official paragraph numbers; objective/achievement mappings still need human confirmation.

The [editable original sources](/fizika/downloads/physics-original-sources.zip) include both languages, platform code, year plans and checks. Original teaching material is CC BY-SA 4.0; code is MIT.
